Ask the 10-K filings

Questions about 20 companies' annual reports. Figures come from a table the system extracted from the filings, with a mark showing how far each one could be checked; explanations are quoted from the filing text, with sources.

Which companies and years can it answer about?

One annual filing per company. A question about another year is answered with the year that is held.
